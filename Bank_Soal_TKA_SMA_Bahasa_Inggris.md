# Bank Soal TKA SMA — Bahasa Inggris (Literasi Bahasa Inggris)

**Jumlah soal:** 300 (nomor 1–300), tersusun dalam 60 teks × 5 soal  
**Acuan:** pola soal literasi bahasa Inggris Ujian Nasional (2020) dan TKA (2025). Seluruh teks dan soal dalam dokumen ini adalah **soal orisinal** yang ditulis mengikuti format, jenis teks, dan level kognitif soal-soal tersebut, bukan salinan soal resmi. Teks dan soal dalam bahasa Inggris; kunci dan pembahasan dalam bahasa Indonesia.

## Bentuk Soal

| Kode | Bentuk | Cara menjawab |
|---|---|---|
| **PG** | Pilihan ganda | Pilih satu jawaban paling tepat (A–E) |

| **BS** | Benar–Salah | Tentukan setiap pernyataan Benar atau Salah |

## Tag Kisi-Kisi

- **[Tekstual]** — menemukan dan memahami informasi tersurat (detail, gagasan utama, rujukan)
- **[Inferensi]** — menyimpulkan informasi tersirat, makna kata dalam konteks, hubungan antargagasan
- **[Evaluasi]** — menilai tujuan penulis, struktur, nada, dan kualitas argumen
- **[Refleksi]** — mengaitkan isi teks dengan pengalaman, nilai, dan situasi lain

## Text 1 — The Return of the Javan Gibbon

> Deep in the cloud forests of West Java lives the Javan gibbon, one of the rarest primates on Earth. Fewer than 4,000 individuals remain, mostly in small forest patches cut off from one another by farms and roads. Gibbons rarely come down to the ground, so a gap in the tree canopy can trap a family group in one area. To solve this problem, conservationists have begun building rope bridges between isolated patches. At first, the animals ignored them. After several months, however, a young female crossed one, and others soon followed. Researchers say the bridges are not a complete solution, but they give the species a chance to find mates and food beyond their small territories.

Therefore, experts advise students to review their notes in the evening, then go to bed on time rather than study through the night.

## Text 4 — The Surprising History of the Pencil

> The pencil seems like a simple tool, but its history is full of surprises. In the 1500s, shepherds in England discovered a soft black mineral that left marks on their sheep. It was later named graphite, although people at first thought it was a type of lead, which explains why we still say "pencil lead". Because graphite was soft and dirty, early writers wrapped it in string or sheepskin. Later, craftsmen in Italy hollowed out wooden sticks and placed graphite inside, creating the pencil we know today. The eraser came much later: for years, people rubbed their mistakes away with bread. It was only in the 1770s that a scientist discovered that natural rubber could rub out pencil marks.

## Text 6 — Why Do We Yawn?

> Yawning is one of the most common behaviours in the animal kingdom, yet scientists still cannot fully explain it. The old idea was that yawning brings extra oxygen to the body, but experiments showed that people yawned just as often in rooms with more oxygen. A newer theory suggests that yawning helps cool the brain. A deep breath draws in cooler air and increases blood flow, which lowers brain temperature. This might explain why people yawn more in warm weather than in freezing cold. Another mystery is that yawning is contagious: seeing someone yawn can make us do the same. Researchers believe this may be linked to empathy, since children under four rarely catch yawns.

## Text 10 — Understanding Inflation

> Inflation is a general rise in prices over time. When inflation is 5 percent a year, something that costs Rp100,000 today will cost about Rp105,000 next year. This means that money buys less than before. Economists say that a small and stable level of inflation is normal in a growing economy, but sudden increases can cause hardship, especially for families who spend most of their income on food. Several factors can cause inflation. When demand for goods grows faster than supply, sellers raise prices. Higher production costs, such as expensive fuel, may also push prices up. To control inflation, central banks usually raise interest rates, which makes borrowing more expensive and encourages people to save rather than spend.
